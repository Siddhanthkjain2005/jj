The first playable uses a custom recruit alongside four authored NPCs. One clean homologation lap or equivalent validated sectors can complete the story; the optional second exhibition is not a barrier. Supported story predicates do not require manual brake/steering ability. Stakes concern a public teaching-room permit, not losing a home after one defeat.

The plan standardizes six core staff, whole-process PSS ≤650 MB plus scene allocation ≤350 MB, cold playable content ≤10 s, cached resume ≤3 s and race load ≤5 s. All budgets are proposed targets, not measurements. A physical Oppo installation exists in project history; no sustained production-performance result is claimed.

The initial account-based India pilot is proposed for consenting adults 18+. A 13+ creative audience is not a legal eligibility rule; teen/family participation and commercial release require the applicable guardian/legal/safety gates. The DPDP commencement timing is described as phased, with relevant section 9 provisions due in May 2027 according to the cited official notification, not already fully commenced at this report date.
