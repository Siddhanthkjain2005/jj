# VelocityVerse privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

VelocityVerse is an offline racing game.

- The game does not ask for any Android permission. It has no access to the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
- The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.
- Your progress (credits, cars, lap times, settings, the driver and team name you type) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.
- Photos and cards you choose to save are written to your own device's Downloads folder. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- We do not collect, share or sell any personal information.
