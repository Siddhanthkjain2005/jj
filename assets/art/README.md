## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: premium racing game's main-menu environment background art, widescreen 16:9, preferably 2560 by 1440.
Primary request: an original breathtaking futuristic motorsport archipelago suspended above clouds at blue-hour sunset. Inspired by the user's floating-world game hub concept, but create a distinctive original world, no licensed brands. Photorealistic high-end cinematic 3D game environment, detailed and sophisticated, inviting and legible.
Composition: panoramic wide shot from an elegant overlook, an immense central circular motorsport headquarters island slightly below center with white metallic architecture, a luminous cyan holographic abstract racing helmet above its atrium, a beautiful original single-seat steel-blue racing car on the central platform. Around it, six substantial satellite islands: upper left a curved city racing circuit and grandstands, lower left a glass-walled engineering workshop with a partly assembled car and robotic arms, far lower left a club pavilion; upper right a lush circuit observatory with an illuminated globe, middle right a driver academy simulator pavilion, lower right an elegant trophy and racing archive gallery. Islands connected by restrained glowing cyan and warm copper road bridges. Background distant mountains and detailed futuristic skyline emerging from rose and indigo clouds. Small scale architectural people allowed for believable scale. Rich physically plausible glass, brushed metal, concrete and lush landscaping. Frame all major islands inside the image, not clipped. Keep uppermost 12 percent predominantly darker sky for interface title and status; leave bottommost 14 percent darker overlook foreground for navigation. Detailed destinations in middle area, moderate contrast in sky, clear atmospheric separation. Cinematic warm sunset rim-light, restrained cyan light paths, deep navy shadows, sophisticated silver, teal and warm amber accents. No large foreground person. No UI panels, no interface, no labels, absolutely no letters, text, logos, watermarks, currency or F1 marks. This will be used as scenic artwork underneath real HTML controls; do not paint the controls.
